Middle Room Training

Learn how to safely and responsibly use the Middle Room before booking.

1. Power & Equipment Safety

  • Only use the labelled power switches — never unplug anything from the wall.
  • If you see green tape or “DO NOT UNPLUG”, leave it exactly as it is.
  • Report damaged plugs, loose cables, or unusual noises immediately.

2. Amplifiers, Pedalboards & Volume Control

  • Do not move or rewire the amplifier stacks, they are set up correctly already.
  • Never pull apart the pedalboards or change patch cables.
  • Keep volume at a safe level at all times.

3. Mixing Desk & Audio Equipment

  • Only adjust the controls you have been shown.
  • Leave faders and knobs where you found them unless instructed.
  • Use headphones when monitoring to avoid feedback.

4. Instruments & Room Care

  • Only take instruments off the wall if you are using them.
  • Return every guitar, bass, or keyboard to its correct stand.
  • Do not leave instruments or cables on the floor.

5. Microphones & Recording Areas

  • Handle microphones carefully, do not swing them by the cable.
  • Do not move drum shields or recording panels.
  • Keep noise reasonable when others are recording.

6. How to Turn the Middle Room On

1. Front Power Button

This button is located at the front of the room. Press it first to power the main equipment rack and speakers.

2. Back Power Button

This button is located at the back of the room. Press it second to activate the mixer, amps, and recording gear.

Both buttons must be turned on for the Middle Room to fully power up. Do not unplug anything or touch taped cables.
